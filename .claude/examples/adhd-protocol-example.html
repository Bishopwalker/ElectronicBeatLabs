SMR Training - Sensorimotor Rhythm Enhancement

🎯 Protocol Purpose

SMR (Sensorimotor Rhythm) training helps improve attention, reduce hyperactivity, and enhance impulse control in ADHD individuals through targeted brainwave entrainment.

Research Base: Sterman & Lubar protocols, validated in clinical studies since the 1970s.

⏱️ Session Details

Total Duration: 30 minutes

Frequency Range: 10-14 Hz (SMR band)

Recommended Usage: Daily sessions for 8-12 weeks

Best Time: Morning or early afternoon

📚 Research Background: SMR training was pioneered by Barry Sterman and Joel Lubar in the 1970s. Clinical studies show 60-80% of ADHD participants experience significant improvement in attention and reduced hyperactivity after 40+ sessions.
⚠️ Important: This is a therapeutic tool, not a medical treatment. Consult with healthcare providers for ADHD management. Use headphones for proper binaural effect. Keep volume at comfortable levels.

🎯 Expected Benefits of SMR Training

  • Improved Attention Span: Increased ability to focus on tasks for extended periods
  • Reduced Hyperactivity: Better impulse control and reduced fidgeting
  • Enhanced Working Memory: Improved ability to hold and manipulate information
  • Better Sleep Quality: More restful sleep patterns and easier sleep initiation
  • Emotional Regulation: Reduced emotional outbursts and better mood stability
  • Academic Performance: Improved grades and classroom behavior
  • Long-term Benefits: Effects often persist months after training completion
